About MecheLab

An independent Australian studio, focused on considered work and lasting client relationships.

Questions, answered.

What kind of businesses do you work with?+

Growing businesses and leadership teams who want clear business and technical advice that leads to clear outcomes. If you are launching, expanding or rethinking your systems, we are likely a good fit.

How does an engagement begin?+

You send an enquiry, we reply promptly and a short call helps us understand your goals. If it feels right, a clear proposal follows.

How long does a project take?+

It depends on the scope and complexity of the work. We agree a clear timeline with you before we start, so you always know what happens next.

How do you price your work?+

Scope and price are agreed upfront, so there are no surprises. Send an enquiry for a tailored quote.

What do you build websites with?+

We choose the platform that best fits your goals and how you plan to update the site, whether that’s a managed website builder or a custom build.

Do you offer support after launch?+

Yes, depending on what we agree together. Some clients prefer to manage their site themselves with training from us, while others like ongoing support, and we’re happy to shape a plan that suits you.
